Our story

Roasted with patience.

Eight years, three cafés and one rule: if we wouldn't drink it, we don't serve it.

2016

A garage, a 5 kg roaster, a lot of notebooks.

Meera and Karan started roasting for friends and a handful of offices. Orders were scribbled on the back of cupping sheets and delivered on a scooter before the morning rush.

A year later the garage was too small, and the first Sector 18 café opened with eleven seats and one grinder.

From farm to cup

How a bean becomes your coffee

1

Source

We visit small estates in Coorg and Chikmagalur every harvest and pay above market.

2

Roast

Small 5 kg batches, profiled by hand, roasted twice a day in Noida.

3

Rest

Every bag rests 4 days, is dated, and is sold within 2 weeks of roasting.

4

Brew

Baristas dial each grinder in daily, tasting every shot before service.
