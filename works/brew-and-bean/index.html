Our story

A roastery that grew a café.

It started as a 5 kg roaster in a Noida garage and a stack of notebooks full of cupping scores. Today we roast twice a day, bake before dawn, and still taste every batch ourselves.

We buy directly from small estates in Coorg and Chikmagalur, pay above-market prices, and print the harvest date on every bag.
