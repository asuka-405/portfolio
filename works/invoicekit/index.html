Questions

Does it handle CGST, SGST and IGST?

Yes, it compares your state with the client's state and applies CGST+SGST for intra-state or IGST for inter-state sales.

Can clients pay online?

Each shared invoice shows a UPI QR for the exact balance. In production you could add payment gateways and auto-reconciliation.

What about TDS?

Choose a TDS rate on the invoice and the balance due is reduced accordingly, so your books match what you actually receive.

Is my data safe?

In this demo, everything stays in your browser. A production version would use encrypted cloud storage with backups.
