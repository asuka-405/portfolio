Care, shipping & returns

How to wash, ship and return your pieces.

Everyday care

  • Wash by hand or on a gentle dishwasher cycle
  • Avoid sudden hot–cold changes
  • Oven safe to 220 °C, microwave safe
  • No steel wool on the glaze

Shipping

  • Free standard shipping over ₹2,000
  • Standard ₹99 · 2–7 days
  • Express ₹249 · 1–4 days
  • Double-boxed, insured

Returns

  • 30 days, unused pieces
  • Refund to original payment
  • Broken in transit? Free replacement
  • Email care@kilnandclay.example
