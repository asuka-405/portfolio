Good to know

Before you book

Do I need experience?

None at all. Our teachers guide you from your first lump of clay.

What should I wear?

Clothes you don't mind getting muddy and short nails. We provide aprons and towels.

When do I get my pieces?

We trim, glaze and fire them for you. Pieces are ready for pickup, or shipped, in about two weeks.
