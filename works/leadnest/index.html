Questions

Can the form go on my own website?

Yes. The enquiry form is a single page you can link to or embed. A production build would also post straight to your database.

Can clients accept a quote online?

In the demo the quote page has an accept button that marks the lead as won. A live build can also record a signature and take an advance by UPI.

Is this connected to Instagram or Justdial?

Not in the demo. A live build can pull enquiries from email alerts or lead forms into the same board.

Is the data real?

No. Leads, quotes and the team are generated for a made-up studio called Bright Interiors.
