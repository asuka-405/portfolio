Questions

Is the credit score real?

No, in this demo a pseudo-score is generated from the PAN and income so you can see the rules engine work. A production build would call a credit bureau.

Can we change the credit policy?

Yes. Minimum score, maximum FOIR, product limits and rates are all configurable and take effect immediately.

Does it handle disbursal and repayments?

The demo creates the loan, shows the amortisation schedule and records EMI payments. Production adds bank-rail disbursal and NACH/UPI mandates.

Is borrower data secure?

In this demo all data stays in your browser. A production system would use encrypted storage, role-based access and audit logs.
