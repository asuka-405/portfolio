Questions

Do my customers need an app?

No. The store opens in any phone browser from your link or QR code, and the order goes to their own WhatsApp.

Is there a commission?

None. You keep 100% of what customers pay you directly, by UPI, cash or however you collect it.

Where is my catalogue stored?

In this demo it lives in your browser, and the share link carries a copy of it. A production version would sync to a database so you can edit from any device.

Can I accept online payments?

The demo shows UPI and cash on delivery. Gateway payments and automatic order confirmation are natural production add-ons.
