Ask about this

Questions

Can tenants pay online?

They pay by UPI from the tenant app and tell you they have paid. You confirm it and the ledger updates. A production build can reconcile automatically with a payment gateway.

How is the deposit refund worked out?

The deposit minus any rent still owed and any damage charges you enter.

Does it handle food plans?

Yes. A food plan adds a monthly amount to the rent for that tenant.

Is the tenant data real?

No. Tenants, rooms and payments are generated for a made-up PG.
