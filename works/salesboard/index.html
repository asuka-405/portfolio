Questions

Is the demo data real?

No, it's generated in your browser for a fictional skincare brand, with realistic seasonality, channel mix and repeat behaviour.

Which tools does it connect to?

The concept shows Shopify, Razorpay, Meta Ads, Google Analytics and Delhivery connectors; the data layer is built to accept any source via CSV or API.

Can I build my own reports?

Yes. The report builder lets you choose a metric, group by time, channel, product, category or region, and save the result.

Can I get alerts by email or WhatsApp?

The rules engine is in the demo; delivery by email, WhatsApp or Slack is a straightforward production add-on.
