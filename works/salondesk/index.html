Questions

Can clients book online without calling?

Yes. The booking page shows only the slots that are actually free for the chosen services and stylist.

Does it handle packages?

Yes. A pack of six facials sits on the client's profile and is used up one session at a time at checkout.

How is commission calculated?

Each stylist has a percentage. The team page shows revenue for the month before GST and tips, and the commission owed.

Is the client data real?

No. Clients and bookings are generated for a made-up salon and stay in your browser.

Book a client, then bill them.
